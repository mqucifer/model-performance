# Spur gear

**Purpose:** a spur gear to mesh with a standard module 1.5 gear, with a true involute tooth profile.

**Size:**
- **Teeth:** 20, module 1.5, a 20° pressure angle, the standard full-depth profile: the pitch circle is 30 mm across, the tips 33 mm (an addendum of one module), the roots 26.25 mm (a dedendum of 1.25 modules). At the pitch circle a tooth and a gap are the same width.
- **Face width:** 8 mm.
- **Bore:** 6 mm, through, on the axis.

**Tolerance:** ±0.1 mm on the tips, the face and the bore's position; ±0.05 mm on the bore's diameter. The tooth's width must follow the involute within about 0.1 mm.

**Placement:** on the Z axis, from Z = 0 to 8, with a tooth centred on +X.
