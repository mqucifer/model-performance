# Vented box

**Purpose:** an open-top box for a small circuit board, with vent slots in its floor.

**Size:**
- **Outside:** 60 mm along X, 40 mm along Y and 30 mm tall.
- **Walls and floor:** 2 mm thick each. The top is open.
- **Vent slots:** five slots through the floor, each 3 mm wide and 30 mm long overall, with round ends. They run along Y and are centred on Y = 0. Their centres are at X = −16, −8, 0, 8 and 16.

**Tolerance:** ±0.1 mm.

**Placement:** the bottom face is centred on the origin, so the box runs from Z = 0 to Z = 30. The opening is at the top.
