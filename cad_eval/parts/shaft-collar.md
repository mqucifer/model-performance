# Shaft collar

**Purpose:** a collar that slides onto an 8 mm shaft and is held by an M3 set screw.

**The shaft it fits:** 8 mm diameter, on the Z axis.

**Size:**
- 18 mm outside diameter, 10 mm long;
- a bore for the shaft, 0.1 to 0.4 mm larger than it on the diameter, about 8.2 mm;
- a 2.5 mm radial hole for the set screw, along +X at Z = 5, from the outside into the bore.

**Tolerance:** ±0.1 mm on the outside; the bore within the clearance above. The collar must never overlap the shaft.

**Placement:** on the shaft's axis, Z, from Z = 0 to Z = 10.
