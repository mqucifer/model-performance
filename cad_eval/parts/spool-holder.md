# Spool holder

I'd like to hang a 1 kg spool of filament on the wall. Its hole is 53 mm across and it's 65 mm wide. Can you make a holder: something it sits on and turns on as the printer pulls the filament?

- It screws to the wall with two 4 mm screws.
- The spool should turn freely, so it can't be a tight fit in the hole.
- The whole width of the spool has to fit on it.
- I'll print it in PLA.

**So it can be checked:** the wall is the plane X = 0, and the holder sticks out from it along +X. The spool's axis is the X axis (Y = 0, Z = 0).
