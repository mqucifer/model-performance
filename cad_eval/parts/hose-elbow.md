# Hose elbow

**Purpose:** a 90° elbow that joins two lengths of 10 mm tube.

**Size:** a tube of 10 mm outside diameter and 7 mm inside diameter, open at both ends. Its centreline:
1. runs straight up from the origin along +Z for 15 mm, to (0, 0, 15);
2. bends 90° towards +X on a radius of 20 mm, about the point (20, 0, 15), to (20, 0, 35);
3. runs straight along +X for 15 mm, to (35, 0, 35).

**Tolerance:** ±0.1 mm.

**Placement:** as given. One open end is centred on the origin, facing −Z. The other is centred on (35, 0, 35), facing +X.
