# Stepped shaft

**Purpose:** a short stepped shaft: a 20 mm collar, a 14 mm bearing seat and an 8 mm end.

**Size:** three cylinders on one axis, end to end:
1. 20 mm diameter, 10 mm long;
2. 14 mm diameter, 20 mm long;
3. 8 mm diameter, 10 mm long, with a 1 mm × 45° chamfer on its free end's outer edge.

The shaft is 40 mm long overall.

**Tolerance:** ±0.1 mm.

**Placement:** the axis is Z. The 20 mm end's face is centred on the origin, so the shaft runs from Z = 0 to Z = 40, with the 8 mm end at the top.
