# Hole plug

**Purpose:** a plug that closes a 20 mm hole in a 5 mm panel, with a head that rests on the panel's top.

**The panel:** 5 mm thick, from Z = 0 to Z = 5, with a 20 mm hole on the Z axis.

**Size:**
- **Head:** 26 mm diameter, 2 mm thick, on the panel's top, from Z = 5 to Z = 7.
- **Shank:** fills the hole through the panel's thickness, from Z = 0 to Z = 5. Its diameter is 0.1 to 0.4 mm smaller than the hole's, about 19.8 mm.

**Tolerance:** ±0.1 mm on the head; the shank within the clearance above. The plug must never overlap the panel.

**Placement:** as fitted, on the Z axis, from Z = 0 to Z = 7.
