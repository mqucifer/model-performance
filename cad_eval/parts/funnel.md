# Funnel

**Purpose:** a small funnel for filling bottles, open at both ends.

**Size:** its walls are 1.5 mm thick, measured horizontally.
- **Spout:** a straight tube from Z = 0 to Z = 10, 12 mm inside diameter and 15 mm outside.
- **Cone:** from the top of the spout at Z = 10 to the rim at Z = 40.
  - Its inside widens in a straight line from 12 mm diameter at Z = 10 to 60 mm at Z = 40.
  - Its outside is 1.5 mm further out horizontally all the way, so 15 mm to 63 mm.

**Tolerance:** ±0.1 mm.

**Placement:** the axis is Z, with the spout's bottom end centred on the origin and the wide rim at the top.
