# L-bracket

**Purpose:** a right-angle bracket that holds a shelf to a wall, with two M5 screws in each leg.

**Size:**
- **Base leg:** flat on the XY plane, 50 mm along X, 40 mm along Y and 4 mm thick (Z = 0 to 4). It runs from X = 0 to X = 50, centred on Y = 0.
- **Upright leg:** stands at X = 0 to 4, the full 40 mm wide, rising to Z = 50.
- **Inside corner:** a fillet of 5 mm radius where the legs meet on the inside, along the full width.
- **Holes:** four through holes of 5.5 mm diameter.
  - Two go through the base leg along Z, at X = 35, Y = +10 and Y = −10.
  - Two go through the upright leg along X, at Z = 35, Y = +10 and Y = −10.

**Tolerance:** ±0.1 mm.

**Placement:** as given, with the outside corner of the L along the Y axis at X = 0, Z = 0.
