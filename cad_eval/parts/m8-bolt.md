# M8 bolt

**Purpose:** a printed M8 bolt that screws into a standard M8 nut: a hex head and a threaded shank.

**Size:**
- **Head:** a hexagon 13 mm across the flats, 5.3 mm tall.
- **Shank:** 20 mm long, threaded along its whole length: M8 × 1.25, right-hand, ISO's 60° metric profile. So 8 mm across the crests, a 1.25 mm pitch, and 6.65 mm across the roots (the basic profile's minor diameter).

**Tolerance:** ±0.1 mm on the head and the length; the crests between 7.8 and 8.0 mm across, the roots within 0.2 mm of the profile's, and the pitch exact.

**Placement:** on the Z axis: the head from Z = -5.3 to 0, with two of its flats parallel to the XZ plane (so it's 13 mm across along Y), and the shank from Z = 0 to 20.
