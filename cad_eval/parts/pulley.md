# V-groove pulley

**Purpose:** a pulley for a round belt, on an 8 mm shaft.

**Size:**
- a disc of 40 mm diameter, 12 mm wide;
- an 8 mm bore through its axis;
- a V-groove all round its rim, centred across its width. The groove is 6 mm wide at the rim and 4 mm deep, with straight sides meeting at the bottom.

**Tolerance:** ±0.1 mm.

**Placement:** the axis is Z, with one face centred on the origin, from Z = 0 to Z = 12.
