# Shelf bracket

I'm putting up a shelf: a board 150 mm deep, and with what goes on it, about 2 kg. It sits on two of these brackets, screwed to the wall. Can you make the bracket?

- It screws to the wall with two 4 mm wood screws, so it needs two holes for them, going into the wall.
- It should hold the shelf most of the way out, at least 100 mm from the wall, but not stick out past it.
- It should sit flat against the wall.
- I'll print it in PLA.

**So it can be checked:** the wall is the plane X = 0, and the bracket is on the room's side of it (X from 0). The shelf rests on the bracket's top, at Z = 0, and the bracket is under it. Centre it on Y = 0.
