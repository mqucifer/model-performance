# Rail slider

**Purpose:** a slider that runs along a rectangular rail, over its top and both sides.

**The rail:** 20 mm wide along Y and 10 mm tall, running along X, from Z = 0 to Z = 10 and centred on Y = 0.

**Size:**
- a block 30 mm along X, 30 mm along Y and 15 mm tall;
- a channel through it along X, open at the bottom, for the rail, with a clearance of 0.2 mm on each side and over the top (0.05 to 0.35 mm allowed). So the channel is 20.4 mm wide and 10.2 mm tall.

**Tolerance:** ±0.1 mm on the block; the channel within the clearance above. The slider must never overlap the rail.

**Placement:** sitting on the rail at X = 0: centred on the origin in X and Y, from Z = 0 to Z = 15.
