# Divided tray

**Purpose:** an open tray for a drawer, split into two compartments.

**Size:**
- 80 mm along X, 60 mm along Y and 20 mm tall outside;
- walls and floor 2 mm thick. The top is open;
- one divider wall, 2 mm thick, across the tray along Y at X = 0, the full height of the tray, joining the walls on both sides.

**Tolerance:** ±0.1 mm.

**Placement:** centred on the origin in X and Y, from Z = 0 to Z = 20.
