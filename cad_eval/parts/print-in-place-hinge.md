# Print-in-place hinge

**Purpose:** a hinge printed in one go, already assembled: two leaves and their knuckles, the pin part of one leaf and captive in the other's knuckle, with a clearance everywhere so it turns straight off the printer.

**Size:**
- **The axis:** along Y, through the origin.
- **Leaf A:** a plate 3 mm thick from X = -25 to -3.3, Y = -15 to 15. Its two knuckles are tubes of 6 mm outside diameter round the axis, from Y = -15 to -5.15 and from Y = 5.15 to 15, each joined to the plate by a bridge from X = -3.3 to -2 over the knuckle's length. A pin of 3 mm diameter runs along the axis from Y = -15 to 15, joined to both knuckles.
- **Leaf B:** a plate 3 mm thick from X = 3.3 to 25, Y = -15 to 15. Its one knuckle is round the axis from Y = -4.85 to 4.85: 6 mm outside, with a 3.6 mm bore for the pin. It is joined to its plate by a bridge from X = 2 to 3.3 over the knuckle's length.
- So there is a 0.3 mm clearance everywhere the leaves meet: round the pin, between the knuckles' ends, and between each knuckle and the other leaf's plate.

**Tolerance:** the clearance must stay between 0.25 and 0.35 mm, and the leaves must not touch: two separate solids. ±0.05 mm on the pin and the bore, ±0.1 mm on everything else.

**Placement:** the plates centred on Z = 0, from Z = -1.5 to 1.5, the knuckles from Z = -3 to 3.
