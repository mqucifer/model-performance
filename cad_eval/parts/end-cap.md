# End cap

**Purpose:** a cap that pushes onto the end of a 10 mm rod.

**Size:**
- a cylinder of 16 mm diameter, 12 mm tall;
- a blind hole of 10 mm diameter, 10 mm deep, into its bottom face, on its axis. The top is closed, 2 mm thick.

**Tolerance:** ±0.1 mm.

**Placement:** the axis is Z. The open, bottom face is centred on the origin, so the cap runs from Z = 0 to Z = 12.
