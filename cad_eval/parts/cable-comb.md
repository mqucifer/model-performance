# Cable comb

The cables behind my desk are a mess. Can you make a comb that keeps six of them side by side? They're all about 5 mm thick.

- Each cable gets its own slot, and clicks in from the top.
- The cables shouldn't be squashed, or flop about either.
- The comb stands on the desk.

**So it can be checked:** the desk is the plane Z = 0, and the comb stands on it. The cables run along X, side by side along Y, with their centres at Z = 10. Centre the comb on X = 0 and Y = 0.
