# Tube clip

I want to keep a broom handle under my desk: it's a tube 20 mm across. Can you make a clip that it snaps into, and that screws up into the underside of the desk?

- It should grip the tube, not let it rattle, and the tube should snap in and pull out by hand.
- One or two 4 mm wood screws into the desk, from below.
- Its top is flat against the desk.

**So it can be checked:** the desk's underside is Z = 0 and the clip hangs below it. The tube runs along X, with its centre 20 mm below the desk, at Y = 0, Z = -20. Centre the clip on X = 0.
