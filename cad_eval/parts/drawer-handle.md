# Drawer handle

**Purpose:** a round bar handle for a drawer, whose two legs screw into the drawer front.

**Size:** a round bar of 8 mm diameter, bent along a path in the XZ plane. The path, at the bar's centre:
1. rises from (−40, 0, 0) to (−40, 0, 20);
2. bends 90° on a 10 mm radius, to (−30, 0, 30);
3. runs straight to (30, 0, 30);
4. bends 90° on a 10 mm radius, to (40, 0, 20);
5. comes down to (40, 0, 0).

The two ends are flat, on Z = 0.

**Tolerance:** ±0.1 mm.

**Placement:** as given.
