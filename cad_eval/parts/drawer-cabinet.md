# Drawer and cabinet

**Purpose:** a small drawer that slides into an open-fronted cabinet, as in a parts organiser: two parts, printed separately, shown with the drawer pushed in.

**The files:** `cabinet.step` and `drawer.step`, each placed as assembled.

**Size:**
- **Cabinet:** a box 100 mm along X, 80 mm deep along Y and 60 mm tall, with 3 mm walls, a floor, a top and a back, and open at the front, Y = 0. So its inside runs from X = -47 to 47, Y = 0 to 77, Z = 3 to 57.
- **Drawer:** an open-topped box with 2 mm walls and a 2 mm floor, 0.5 mm clear of the cabinet's inside on each side, the floor and the back: so it runs from X = -46.5 to 46.5, Y = -0.5 to 76.5, Z = 3.5 to 50.
- **The drawer's front:** a plate on the drawer's front 100 mm wide (X = -50 to 50), 3 mm thick, from Y = -3.5 to -0.5, and from Z = 0.5 to 59.5. So it stops 0.5 mm short of the cabinet's front edge. It has a finger hole 20 mm across through it and the drawer's front wall, along Y, centred at X = 0, Z = 40.

**Tolerance:** the drawer's gap to the cabinet, everywhere, between 0.4 and 0.6 mm, never overlapping; ±0.1 mm on everything else.

**Placement:** as assembled, as above: the cabinet from X = -50 to 50, Y = 0 to 80, Z = 0 to 60.
