# Tube coupler

**Purpose:** a sleeve that joins two 10 mm tubes end to end, with a stop in the middle that each tube butts against.

**The tubes:** 10 mm outside and 7 mm inside diameter, on the Z axis. One runs from Z = −20 to −0.5, the other from Z = 0.5 to 20.

**Size:**
- a sleeve 14 mm outside diameter and 30 mm long;
- a bore for the tubes, 0.1 to 0.4 mm larger than them on the diameter, about 10.2 mm;
- a stop ring inside it at the middle, from Z = −0.5 to 0.5, with a 7 mm hole, matching the tubes' inside.

**Tolerance:** ±0.1 mm on the outside and the stop; the bore within the clearance above. The coupler must never overlap the tubes.

**Placement:** centred on the origin, along Z, from Z = −15 to Z = 15.
