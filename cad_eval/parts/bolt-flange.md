# Bolt flange

**Purpose:** a round flange that bolts a 30 mm pipe to a panel.

**Size:**
- a disc of 80 mm diameter, 6 mm thick;
- a 30 mm hole in the middle;
- six 6.5 mm holes, equally spaced on a 60 mm bolt circle. The first is on the +X axis, at (30, 0).

**Tolerance:** ±0.1 mm.

**Placement:** the axis is Z. The bottom face is centred on the origin, from Z = 0 to Z = 6.
