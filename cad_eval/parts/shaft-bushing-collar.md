# Shaft, bushing and collar

**Purpose:** a shaft turning in a flanged bushing, kept from sliding out by a collar with a set screw: three parts, printed separately and assembled.

**The files:** `shaft.step`, `bushing.step` and `collar.step`, each placed as assembled, on the Z axis.

**Size:**
- **Shaft:** 8 mm across, from Z = -10 to 50.
- **Bushing:** a flange 18 mm across and 2 mm thick, from Z = 0 to 2, and a sleeve 12 mm across from Z = 2 to 12. Its bore is 8.3 mm, through.
- **Collar:** 18 mm across, with a bore of 8.3 mm, from Z = 12.3 to 20.3: 0.3 mm above the bushing. A hole for an M3 set screw, 2.5 mm across, runs from the bore out along +X, at Z = 16.3.

**Tolerance:** ±0.05 mm on the shaft and the bores, so the shaft's clearance in each bore is 0.1 to 0.2 mm; the collar's gap to the bushing 0.25 to 0.35 mm; ±0.1 mm on everything else. No two parts may overlap.

**Placement:** as assembled, as above.
