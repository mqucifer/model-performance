# Hex standoff

**Purpose:** a hexagonal standoff that holds a board 15 mm off a panel, on an M3 screw through its middle.

**Size:**
- a hexagonal prism 8 mm across the flats and 15 mm long;
- a 3.2 mm through hole along its axis.

**Tolerance:** ±0.1 mm.

**Placement:**
- The axis is Z, from Z = 0 to Z = 15, centred on the origin.
- Two of its flats are parallel to the XZ plane, so the flats are at Y = ±4 and two corners lie on the X axis.
