# Press-fit pin

**Purpose:** a dowel pin pressed into a hole in a printed block, held by interference alone, with no glue.

**The block it goes into:** 20 by 20 mm and 10 mm tall, centred on the Z axis from Z = 0 to 10, with a 5.00 mm hole straight through along Z, on the axis.

**Size:**
- **The pin:** 5.10 mm across and 16 mm long: 0.10 mm bigger than the hole, so it presses in.
- **Chamfers:** a 0.5 mm by 45° chamfer at each end, so it starts straight.

**Tolerance:** ±0.02 mm on the pin's diameter, so the interference stays between 0.08 and 0.12 mm; ±0.1 mm on its length and chamfers.

**Placement:** as pressed in: on the Z axis, its bottom flush with the block's bottom, from Z = 0 to Z = 16.
