# Snap-fit enclosure

**Purpose:** a box and a lid that snaps onto it, with no screws: two cantilever hooks on the lid catch in windows in the box's walls. Two parts, shown snapped shut.

**The files:** `box.step` and `lid.step`, each placed as assembled.

**Size:**
- **Box:** 60 mm along X, 40 mm along Y and 30 mm tall, with 2 mm walls and a 2 mm floor, open at the top. Each of its two end walls (at X = ±30) has a window through it: 12 mm wide along Y (Y = -6 to 6) and 4 mm tall, from Z = 21 to 25.
- **Lid:** a plate 63.6 mm along X (X = -31.8 to 31.8, so it reaches over the arms), 40 mm along Y and 2 mm thick, resting on the box's rim, from Z = 30 to 32. Two arms hang down from its ends, one outside each end wall, 0.2 mm clear of the wall: each 1.6 mm thick, 10 mm wide along Y (Y = -5 to 5), from Z = 20 to 30. So the arm at +X runs from X = 30.2 to 31.8, and the one at -X from -31.8 to -30.2.
- **Hooks:** on each arm's inside face, pointing into the window: 1.0 mm deep from the arm, so 0.8 mm into the wall's thickness. Each hook's top is flat, at Z = 24.8, 0.2 mm under the window's top, and it ramps back to the arm at Z = 21.8: a right triangle in the XZ section, across the arm's width.

**Tolerance:** the parts mustn't overlap, and the lid rests on the rim. Each hook must reach into its window, under the window's top edge. ±0.05 mm on the arms and hooks, ±0.1 mm on everything else.

**Placement:** as assembled: the box centred on the Z axis from Z = 0 to 30, the lid on it.
