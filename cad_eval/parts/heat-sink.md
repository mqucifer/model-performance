# Heat sink

**Purpose:** a finned heat sink for a 40 mm chip.

**Size:**
- **Base:** 40 mm square, 3 mm thick.
- **Fins:** seven straight fins on the base, each 1.5 mm thick and 15 mm tall, running the full 40 mm along Y. Their centres are at X = −18, −12, −6, 0, 6, 12 and 18.

The heat sink is 18 mm tall overall.

**Tolerance:** ±0.1 mm.

**Placement:** centred on the origin in X and Y, with the base's underside on Z = 0.
