# Slotted bar

**Purpose:** a flat bar with an adjustment slot, so a bolt through it can slide.

**Size:**
- 80 mm along X, 12 mm along Y and 5 mm thick;
- one through slot along X, centred on the bar, 5.5 mm wide and 40 mm long overall, with round ends.

**Tolerance:** ±0.1 mm.

**Placement:** centred on the origin in X and Y, from Z = 0 to Z = 5.
