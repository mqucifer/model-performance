# Coil spring

**Purpose:** a printed coil spring, for a light-duty return.

**Size:**
- a round wire of 2 mm diameter, coiled in a right-handed helix;
- the coil is 20 mm across, measured at the wire's centre (a 10 mm radius);
- a pitch of 5 mm, for four full turns, so the wire's centre rises 20 mm.

**Tolerance:** ±0.1 mm.

**Placement:**
- The axis is Z.
- The wire's centre starts at (10, 0, 0) and turns counter-clockwise seen from +Z, ending at (10, 0, 20).
- Each end of the wire is cut square to it.
