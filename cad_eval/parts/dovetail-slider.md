# Dovetail slider

**Purpose:** a slider that runs along a dovetail rail and can't lift off it, as on a camera mount or a printer's tool changer. It slides with a small, even clearance.

**The rail it rides on:** along X, from X = -30 to 30. Its base is 20 mm wide (Y = -10 to 10) from Z = 0 to 5. On top is the dovetail tongue, a trapezoid in the YZ section: 10 mm wide at Z = 5 (Y = -5 to 5), widening to 14 mm at its top, Z = 10 (Y = -7 to 7).

**Size:**
- **Block:** 30 mm along X, 24 mm wide along Y; it sits 0.2 mm above the rail's base and is 15 mm tall at the top, Z = 15.
- **Groove:** the dovetail groove along its whole length, the tongue's shape with 0.2 mm of clearance on every face: 0.2 mm off each slanted flank (measured square to it) and 0.2 mm above the tongue's top.

**Tolerance:** the clearance must stay between 0.15 and 0.25 mm on every face, and the slider must never overlap the rail; ±0.1 mm on the block.

**Placement:** as fitted on the rail: centred on X = 0 and Y = 0, from Z = 5.2 to Z = 15.
