# Spacer

**Purpose:** a round spacer that sits between a board and its case on an M5 screw.

**Size:**
- outside diameter 12 mm;
- a through hole of 5.3 mm diameter (M5 clearance), on the same axis;
- 10 mm long.

**Tolerance:** ±0.1 mm on every dimension.

**Placement:** the axis is Z. The bottom face is centred on the origin, so the spacer runs from Z = 0 to Z = 10.
