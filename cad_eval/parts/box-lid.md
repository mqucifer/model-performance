# Box lid

**Purpose:** a lid that press-fits onto an open-top box, with a lip that goes inside the box's opening.

**The box it fits:** 60 mm along X, 40 mm along Y and 30 mm tall outside, with 2 mm walls. It is centred on the origin, from Z = 0 to Z = 30, open at the top. So the opening is 56 mm by 36 mm.

**Size:**
- **Top plate:** 60 by 40 mm and 2 mm thick, resting on the box's rim, from Z = 30 to Z = 32.
- **Lip:** a rectangular ring under the plate, going into the opening.
  - It is 4 mm deep, from Z = 26 to Z = 30, with walls 1.5 mm thick.
  - Its outside leaves 0.2 mm of clearance to the box's inside walls on every side, so it is 55.6 by 35.6 mm outside.

**Tolerance:** ±0.1 mm on the plate and the lip's wall. The clearance must stay between 0.05 and 0.35 mm, and the lid must never overlap the box.

**Placement:** as fitted on the box: centred on the Z axis, from Z = 26 to Z = 32.
