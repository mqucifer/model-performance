# Flanged bushing

**Purpose:** a bushing for an 8 mm shaft, with a flange that stops it in its housing.

**Size:**
- **Flange:** 20 mm diameter, 3 mm thick.
- **Body:** 12 mm diameter, rising from the flange to a total height of 15 mm.
- **Bore:** 8 mm diameter, all the way through.

**Tolerance:** ±0.1 mm.

**Placement:** the axis is Z. The flange's bottom face is centred on the origin, so the flange is Z = 0 to 3 and the body Z = 3 to 15.
