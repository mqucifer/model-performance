# Cup

**Purpose:** a round cup for small parts on a workbench.

**Size:**
- 50 mm outside diameter and 40 mm tall;
- walls 2 mm thick and the floor 3 mm thick. The top is open.

**Tolerance:** ±0.1 mm.

**Placement:** the axis is Z. The bottom face is centred on the origin, so the cup runs from Z = 0 to Z = 40.
