# U-channel

**Purpose:** a short U-shaped channel that holds a cable run to a shelf, with two screws through its floor.

**Size:**
- 60 mm long along X;
- 30 mm wide outside along Y, and 20 mm tall;
- floor and both walls 3 mm thick. The top is open;
- two 4.5 mm through holes in the floor, on the centreline, at X = 15 and X = 45.

**Tolerance:** ±0.1 mm.

**Placement:** it runs from X = 0 to X = 60, centred on Y = 0, with the floor's underside on Z = 0.
