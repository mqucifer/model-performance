# Snap-fit hook

**Purpose:** a cantilever snap-fit hook that latches under a ledge on a panel, as on a battery cover. The beam bends as it goes on, and the hook's flat back holds it there.

**The panel it latches to:** a block from X = 5.1 to 10, Y = -6 to 6, Z = 10 to 16.9. Its face at X = 5.1 is what the beam runs along, and its top at Z = 16.9 is the ledge the hook catches.

**Size:**
- **Base:** 10 mm along X, 8 mm along Y, 4 mm tall.
- **Beam:** stands on the base's top, at its +X edge: 1.6 mm thick along X, the base's full 8 mm wide, and 16 mm tall above the base.
- **Hook:** at the beam's top, sticking out 1.0 mm in +X. Its underside is flat (the catch) at 3 mm below the beam's top, and it ramps back to the beam's face at the beam's top: a right triangle in the XZ section, extruded across the beam's width.

**Tolerance:** ±0.05 mm on the beam's thickness and the hook's overhang; ±0.1 mm on everything else. It must not overlap the panel, and its gap to it, at the beam's face and under the hook, is 0.1 mm (0.05 to 0.15 mm).

**Placement:** as latched: the base centred on the Z axis from Z = 0 to 4, so it runs from X = -5 to 5, and the beam from X = 3.4 to 5, from Z = 4 to 20. The hook's catch is at Z = 17, 0.1 mm above the ledge.
