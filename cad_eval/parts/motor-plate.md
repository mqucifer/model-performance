# Motor plate

**Purpose:** a plate that mounts a NEMA 17 stepper motor.

**Size:**
- 60 mm square and 4 mm thick, with the four corners rounded to a 5 mm radius (the edges along Z);
- a 22 mm hole in the middle for the motor's boss;
- four 3.4 mm holes on a 31 mm square around it, at X = ±15.5 and Y = ±15.5.

**Tolerance:** ±0.1 mm.

**Placement:** centred on the origin in X and Y, from Z = 0 to Z = 4.
