# Bearing housing

**Purpose:** a block that holds a 608 ball bearing (22 mm outside, 7 mm wide), screwed to a panel.

**The bearing it holds:** 22 mm diameter and 7 mm wide, on the Z axis, sitting from Z = 2 to Z = 9.

**Size:**
- a block 40 mm square and 10 mm tall;
- a pocket for the bearing from the top face down to Z = 2, its diameter 0.05 to 0.25 mm larger than the bearing's, about 22.1 mm;
- a 16 mm through hole under the pocket, for the shaft and the bearing's inner race;
- four 4.5 mm mounting holes through the block, at X = ±15 and Y = ±15.

**Tolerance:** ±0.1 mm on the block and holes; the pocket within the clearance above. The housing must never overlap the bearing.

**Placement:** centred on the origin in X and Y, from Z = 0 to Z = 10.
