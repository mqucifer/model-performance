# Caster wheel

**Purpose:** a small fixed caster: a wheel turning on an axle in a fork, kept centred by two spacers. Five parts, printed separately and assembled.

**The files:** `fork.step`, `wheel.step`, `axle.step`, `spacer-a.step` and `spacer-b.step`, each placed as assembled. The axle runs along Y, through the origin.

**Size:**
- **Axle:** 5 mm across, from Y = -14 to 14.
- **Wheel:** 40 mm across and 12 mm wide, from Y = -6 to 6, with a 5.3 mm bore.
- **Spacers:** tubes 9 mm across outside with a 5.3 mm bore, from Y = -9 to -6.3 (`spacer-a`) and from Y = 6.3 to 9 (`spacer-b`): 0.3 mm clear of the wheel.
- **Fork:** two arms, each 16 mm along X (X = -8 to 8) and 4 mm thick, from Y = -13.3 to -9.3 and from Y = 9.3 to 13.3 (0.3 mm clear of the spacers), from Z = -8 to 26, each with a 5.3 mm hole for the axle; and a top plate joining them, 24 mm along X, from Y = -13.3 to 13.3 and from Z = 26 to 30.

**Tolerance:** ±0.05 mm on the axle and the bores and holes, so the axle's clearance in each is 0.1 to 0.2 mm; the gaps between the wheel, the spacers and the fork 0.25 to 0.35 mm; ±0.1 mm on everything else. No two parts may overlap.

**Placement:** as assembled, as above.
