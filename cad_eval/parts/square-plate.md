# Mounting plate

**Purpose:** a square plate that mounts a small board on four M3 screws.

**Size:**
- 50 mm square and 3 mm thick;
- four through holes of 3.4 mm diameter (M3 clearance), one near each corner, at X = ±20 and Y = ±20.

**Tolerance:** ±0.1 mm.

**Placement:** centred on the origin in X and Y, from Z = 0 to Z = 3.
