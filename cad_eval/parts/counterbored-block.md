# Counterbored block

**Purpose:** a mounting block held down by two M4 socket-head screws whose heads sit below its top face.

**Size:**
- a block 40 mm along X, 20 mm along Y and 15 mm tall;
- two holes, at X = ±12 on the centreline, each:
  - a 4.5 mm through hole;
  - an 8 mm counterbore, 4.5 mm deep from the top face.

**Tolerance:** ±0.1 mm.

**Placement:** centred on the origin in X and Y, from Z = 0 to Z = 15.
