# Living hinge

**Purpose:** two flaps joined by a thin, flexible web, printed flat, that folds without a pin: a living hinge, as on a box's snap-shut lid. The web is what flexes, so its thickness matters more than anything else.

**Size:**
- **Two flaps:** each 30 mm along X, 20 mm along Y and 3 mm thick, with a 4 mm gap between them along X.
- **The web:** joins the flaps across that gap, along their whole 20 mm width. It is 0.4 mm thick, flush with the flaps' bottom faces.

**Tolerance:** ±0.05 mm on the web's thickness; ±0.1 mm on everything else.

**Placement:** flat on Z = 0, centred on the origin: the flaps from X = -32 to -2 and from X = 2 to 32, Y from -10 to 10, Z from 0 to 3. The web runs from X = -2 to 2, from Z = 0 to 0.4.
