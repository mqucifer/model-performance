# Wall hook

Can you make a hook for my hallway to hang a bag on? The bag weighs up to 5 kg, and its strap is about 40 mm wide, so the hook needs room for it.

- It screws to the wall with two 4 mm screws.
- The strap shouldn't slide off the end.
- I'll print it in PLA.

**So it can be checked:** the wall is the plane X = 0, and the hook is on the room's side of it (X from 0). The strap hangs over the hook with its width along Y. Centre it on Y = 0 and Z = 0.
