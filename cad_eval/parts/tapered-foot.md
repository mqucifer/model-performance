# Tapered foot

**Purpose:** a tapered foot for a piece of furniture, fixed by a screw through its middle.

**Size:**
- a square frustum, 30 mm tall, with flat, straight sides:
  - 40 mm square at the bottom;
  - 20 mm square at the top;
- a 6.5 mm through hole along its axis.

**Tolerance:** ±0.1 mm.

**Placement:** the axis is Z, with the bottom face centred on the origin and its edges parallel to X and Y, from Z = 0 to Z = 30.
