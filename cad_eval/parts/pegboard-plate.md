# Pegboard plate

**Purpose:** a small pegboard panel for hanging tools.

**Size:**
- 60 mm along X, 40 mm along Y and 3 mm thick;
- a grid of 5 mm through holes on a 10 mm pitch: five columns at X = −20, −10, 0, 10 and 20, and three rows at Y = −10, 0 and 10. That's 15 holes.

**Tolerance:** ±0.1 mm.

**Placement:** centred on the origin in X and Y, from Z = 0 to Z = 3.
