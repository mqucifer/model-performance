# Bolt and nut

**Purpose:** a printed M8 bolt with its nut screwed on: the threads must mesh with a clearance, so the nut turns. Two parts.

**The files:** `bolt.step` and `nut.step`, each placed as assembled.

**Size:**
- **Bolt:** a hex head 13 mm across the flats and 5.3 mm tall, from Z = -5.3 to 0, and a shank threaded along its whole length from Z = 0 to 30: M8 × 1.25, right-hand, ISO's 60° metric profile, 8 mm across the crests and 6.65 mm across the roots (the basic profile's minor diameter).
- **Nut:** a hexagon 13 mm across the flats and 6.5 mm thick, from Z = 10 to 16.5, threaded inside to match: the same profile, 0.15 mm bigger everywhere across its radius, so it runs on with a clearance.
- **Both heads** have two flats parallel to the XZ plane, so they're 13 mm across along Y.

**Tolerance:** the threads mustn't overlap, and their closest distance, flank to flank, is between 0.05 and 0.2 mm. ±0.1 mm on the hexagons and lengths; the pitch exact.

**Placement:** as assembled, on the Z axis, as above.
