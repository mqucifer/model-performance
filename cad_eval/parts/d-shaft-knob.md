# Knob for a D-shaft

I need a new knob for my lamp's dimmer. The shaft is the usual 6 mm D-shaft: round, with a flat on one side, 4.5 mm across from the flat to the far side. It sticks out 12 mm from the panel.

- It should push onto the shaft snugly and turn it, without slipping on the flat.
- It should go well down the shaft, at least 8 mm.
- It shouldn't rub on the panel.
- Something easy to grip and turn, a normal knob size.

**So it can be checked:** the panel is the plane Z = 0 and the knob is in front of it (Z from 0). The shaft is on the Z axis from Z = 0 to 12, with its flat facing +X: the flat is the plane X = 1.5.
